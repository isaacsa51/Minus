Minus is a familiar calculator-style budget tracking app for Android.

It focuses on making daily spending easy to record with a simple numpad interface, quick calculations, local expense history, and practical tools for managing budgets and recurring expenses.

Core features:
- Log expenses quickly through a calculator-style numpad.
- Track budgets, daily averages, subscriptions, and recurring expenses.
- Export expense data as CSV for backup or spreadsheet analysis.
- Undo entries and manage multiple expenses efficiently.
- Customize the experience with light, dark, system theme, Material You colors, and typography options.
- Use home screen widgets for quick expense views and actions.
- Pair with the Wear OS companion app for fast expense entry from your watch.

Minus is built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material Design 3, Room, Hilt, and modern Android development practices.
