<p>Minus is a familiar calculator-style budget tracking app for Android.</p><p>It focuses on making daily spending easy to record with a simple numpad interface, quick calculations, local expense history, and practical tools for managing budgets and recurring expenses.</p><p><b>Core features:</b></p><ul><li>Log expenses quickly through a calculator-style numpad.</li><li>Track budgets, daily averages, subscriptions, and recurring expenses.</li><li>Export expense data as CSV for backup or spreadsheet analysis.</li><li>Undo entries and manage multiple expenses efficiently.</li><li>Customize the experience with light, dark, system theme, Material You colors, and typography options.</li><li>Use home screen widgets for quick expense views and actions.</li><li>Pair with the Wear OS companion app for fast expense entry from your watch.</li></ul><p>Minus is built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material Design 3, Room, Hilt, and modern Android development practices.</p>
